# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome questions and constructive
criticism, focus feedback on the work, and assume good intent.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable in project issues, discussions, pull requests, or
other project spaces. Do not publish another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock discussions, and temporarily
or permanently restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and
applied consistently, including to maintainers.

Report abusive public content using GitHub's reporting tools. For sensitive
matters, ask a maintainer for a private channel without posting personal details.
A dedicated private conduct-reporting contact must be chosen before public
launch; see the [publication checklist](docs/OPEN_SOURCE_READINESS.md).
