# Code of conduct

Be respectful. Welcome questions and constructive criticism, keep feedback about the work, and assume good intent.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption are not allowed in issues, discussions, pull requests, or any other project space. Don't share anyone's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, and restrict participation temporarily or permanently. Decisions are meant to be proportionate and applied consistently, including to maintainers.

Report abusive content with GitHub's reporting tools. For sensitive matters, ask a maintainer for a private channel without including personal details.
